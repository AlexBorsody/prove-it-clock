# Prove Value V1: Marketing and Launch Brief

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: draft for Alex's review

## The V1 call (locked)

- V1 is free. No paywall on verdicts, methodology, timelines, or embeds.
- The monetization strategy is deferred. The "special sauce" (valuation model, Prove Value Index, Pro/API tiers) ships later, and community input is part of building it well.
- The V1 game is SEO + links. The index is the asset: verdicts, promise-level pages, and methodology become indexable content nobody else has, and embed cards become the link machine.

This fits the moat thesis: ledger history and the link graph compound over time and cannot be bought back. Paid access later sells data, never scores.

## Positioning

- One line: "If projects prove value they should be worth money. We check the receipts."
- The marketing story is the gap between what a project proved and what the market priced it at.
- Honest constraints from day one: verdicts are promise-only, no price in the score; disclosures of the publisher's own holdings stay public on every verdict; no timestamps that imply a track record we do not have; no em dashes in public copy.

## SEO architecture (V1 scope)

1. **Verdict publications are indexable pages.** Each reviewed verdict gets a stable canonical URL with ClaimReview-style structured data (claim, claimant, review verdict, evidence links). This is content no aggregator has.
2. **Promise-level deep links.** Every promise links to the exact place it was stated and gets its own anchor/URL. Promise pages surface in long-tail search ("did X project promise Y").
3. **Methodology as a content hub.** Hearts methodology, the qualification-vs-fulfillment split, the inheritance rule, category benchmarks: all indexable, all citing the evidence ledger.
4. **Technical basics.** Sitemap, clean canonicals, fast loads, coin pages server-rendered. No client-only content on indexable pages.

## Links: the embed card machine

1. **Embeddable verdict cards.** A lightweight snippet any blog, newsletter, or forum can drop in: promise summary, hearts, verdict, with an attribution link back to the canonical verdict page. Distribution that compounds.
2. **Outreach list.** Crypto journalists, analysts, newsletter writers, podcast hosts who cover "is this project real" stories. Each verdict ships with a one-page press kit: the verdict, the top 3 pieces of evidence, the market-cap gap.
3. **Founder/protocol outreach.** Offer projects a free verdict review process. Corrections flow through the evidence ledger, which is itself a story (public, inspectable corrections = trust).

## Content flywheel

- **Per-coin verdict threads.** Each published verdict gets a thread (X, and syndicated to the blog): the promises, the evidence, the verdict, the gap vs market cap.
- **Verdict-change digest.** When a verdict moves (new evidence, time decay, lapsed promises), that is news. Weekly cadence.
- **Methodology explainers.** Short pieces on each hard call: why qualification is separate from fulfillment, why the inheritance rule exists, why verdicts stay price-free. These double as SEO and as the trust story.
- **Time-decay series.** The one-year decay pass is a content event: "what expired this year."

## Launch sequence (draft)

1. Finish promise research for the remaining in-flight projects (XRP 2024/2026 ODL verification; ETH, SOL, DASH, AVAX dossiers to case studies to publish).
2. Ship the claims-and-evidence ledger direction (separate brief).
3. Launch page: rankings go straight to the instrument, methodology one tab away, embed snippet documented.
4. First verdict publication + press kit; outreach wave 1.
5. Embed cards live; track installs.

## What success looks like (V1)

- Referring domains to verdict pages and methodology hub.
- Embed card installs on third-party sites.
- Indexed promise/verdict pages ranking for project-name + "promises"/"verdict" queries.
- Inbound correction requests from projects (signal the instrument is being taken seriously).

Explicitly NOT a V1 metric: revenue.

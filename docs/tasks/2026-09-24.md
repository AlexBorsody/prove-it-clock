## Review findings for the case studies

1. **Allowance resets need an explicit interpretation.** In `hearts-algorithm.md`,
   a new fulfillment resets `S(t)` and restores allowance to `A0`, even after it
   had drained to zero. For example, `A0=2`, six quiet years gives allowance zero;
   a new fulfillment restores two allowance hearts in addition to its reward.
   “Starting allowance” can therefore recur. Also specify whether reward-zero
   lineages or the core gate reset `S(t)`; otherwise trivial delivery can restore
   allowance despite earning zero. Record Alex's interpretation rather than
   silently modifying the formula or choosing which fulfillments count.
2. **Displayed accounting can exceed filled hearts.** Capacity/core clipping
   means earned plus allowance can exceed the displayed total. Example: capacity
   five, earned four, allowance one, core open produces filled four. The UI must
   explain the clipped heart instead of showing a split that appears to sum to
   five. The adopted cap stays; its explanation needs a defined contract.
3. **Retirement is documented but not supported by the old state mirror.**
   `app/src/lib/milestone-states.ts` makes fulfilled terminal. The SQL milestone
   record stores achieved/achieved_at rather than dated lineage transitions.
   A future implementation must preserve both fulfillment and retirement events;
   changing a current-state boolean cannot reproduce the required historical rise
   and fall. The algorithm's zero-heart wording also needs to include the case
   where all previously earned hearts were retired.
4. **Historical provenance must be honest.** Present-tense allowance evidence
   cannot be applied to inception as if it was known then. Specify assessment
   dates and label retrospective case studies reconstructed. Existing history
   semantics infer “observed” from promise publication dates alone; that does not
   prove the assessment or evidence was actually recorded at the historical time.
5. **Blinding is not achievable by this already-exposed reviewer.** This task
   has seen the illustrative scores. A genuinely blinded researcher needs a
   separate brief containing rules and primary-source research instructions but
   excluding worked examples/targets. Do not claim a full-repository reader is
   blind. No researcher was dispatched in this review.

Locked capacity tiers and provisional grace/decay constants remain unchanged.
These findings concern mechanical interpretation and provenance, not a request
to reopen the constants or expand scope.

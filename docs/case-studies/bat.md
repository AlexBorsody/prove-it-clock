# BAT — evidence worksheet

**2026-09-24 · retrospective, non-blinded research · review required.**
Criteria/rewards are recorded in [revision 1](README.md). No production rating.
This is a bounded first pass through major lineages, not a complete roadmap audit.

## What the evidence supports

BAT has shipped a rewarded-advertising mechanism and creator contributions.
That is distinct from proving superior ecosystem economics, universal access or
material token value capture. A simple “nothing delivered” interpretation is
inconsistent with the documented releases below. Issuer release claims still
need functional corroboration; they are not independent audits.

## Present-tense allowance assessment

Window: 2025-09-24 through 2026-09-24. The original 2025-08-14 launch is outside
it; its 2026 page update alone is not proof of new shipping or continuing usage.

| Checklist item | Candidate evidence | Review status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Working product used for stated purpose | B09 reports current BAT integration; B10 reports purchases | Plausible, but direct payout/use corroboration remains |
| Identifiable entity actively shipping | B09 describes current integrations | Candidate yes; distinguish launched functionality from future partner plans |
| Measurable economic activity | B10 dated BAT purchase | Candidate yes only if this measure qualifies; not a proxy for verified ad revenue |

With two or more accepted checklist items, the adopted capacity-10 cap gives
`A0 = 2`. This is a current assessment, not an allowance to copy into 2017 history.
No settled `A0` is asserted until the evidence threshold is agreed.

## Conditional arithmetic for reviewers

These are **scenario checks, not score estimates or a confidence interval**.
They assume accepted current operation, no full retirement, a core still open,
and the stated candidate weights. Unknown lineages make the inventory incomplete.

| Assumptions | Mechanical result at 2026-09-24 |
| --- | --- |
| Accept BAT-1, BAT-2 and independent BAT-4; `A0=2`; last fulfillment 2025-08-14 | Earned `2+1+1=4`; `S<2`; allowance `2`; filled `min(9,4+2)=6` |
| Accept BAT-1 and BAT-2 only; treat BAT-4 as the same lineage with no new fulfillment; no later qualifying fulfillment; `A0=2` | Last known accepted fulfillment 2019-04-24; `7<S<8`; allowance `max(0,2−5)=0`; earned/filled `3` |

If a same-lineage upgrade is allowed to refresh `S`, the second scenario changes.
That is precisely why the lineage/reset decision must precede a displayed number.
No historical daily curve is generated: historical allowance,
source vintages and retirement semantics remain unresolved.
